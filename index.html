About

I am a Research Data Scientist at Google (Core ML Data). I am a core contributor to the Google Gemini team, where I focus on LLM post-training, frontier safety, and automated evaluation quality.

Recently, I led the evaluation data quality and automated loss analysis for the Gemini 2.5 and 3.0 launches, improving model performance on long-context financial understanding and refactoring safety auto-rater pipelines. My work on real-time noise identification in LLM ratings was awarded the 2026 DeepMind Data Science Breakthrough Team Award.
