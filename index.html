Biography

I am a Research Data Scientist at OpenAI. I recently joined OpenAI after a transformative tenure at Google Gemini (Core ML Data), where I was a core contributor to Gemini 2.5 and 3.0. My work focuses on bridging the gap between intricate algorithms and human-aligned AI safety.

I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Stanford University, advised by Professors Peter Glynn and Jose Blanchet. My research expertise includes RLHF/RLAIF, automated evaluation refactoring, and frontier safety for large language models.
