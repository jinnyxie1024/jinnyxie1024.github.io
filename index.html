Jin Xie

Research Data Scientist @ Google Core ML AI Data

I am a Research Data Scientist at Google (Core ML AI Data), focusing on Gemini post-training, frontier safety, and evaluation frameworks. I was a core contributor to Gemini 2.5. My research focuses on building methods that make next-generation AI systems safer and more reliable. Before moving into LLM safety and evaluation, I spent nearly a decade building AI models for high-impact healthcare applications, including early cancer detection, surgical scheduling, and medical device resource allocation.

Previously, I held research and scientist roles at Facebook Research, Microsoft Research, Grail (IPO 2024), Uber, Stanford School of Medicine, and Stanford Hospital.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Stanford University, where I was advised by Prof. Peter Glynn and Prof. Jose Blanchet. I did research in earlier years in quantum chemistry and HPC with Prof. Lin Lin at UC Berkeley and Prof. Huijun Fan at Peking University. Please see my mathematician academic tree below.
