Jin Xie

Research Data Scientist @ Google Core ML AI Data

I am a Research Data Scientist at Google (Core ML AI Data), focusing on Gemini post-training, frontier safety, and evaluation frameworks. I was a core contributor to the Gemini 2.5 report. My research focuses on building methods that make next-generation AI systems safer, more reliable, and more useful in real-world deployment. Before moving into LLM safety and evaluation, I spent nearly a decade building AI models for high-impact healthcare applications, including healthcare system management and early cancer detection.

Previously, I held research and scientist roles at Facebook Research, Microsoft Research, Grail (IPO 2024), Uber, Stanford School of Medicine, and Stanford Hospital.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Stanford University, where I was advised by Prof. Peter Glynn and Prof. Jose Blanchet. I did research in earlier years in quantum chemistry and HPC with Prof. Lin Lin at UC Berkeley and Prof. Huijun Fan at Peking University.
